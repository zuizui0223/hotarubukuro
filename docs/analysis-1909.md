# Active 1,909-observation analysis

## Status

The 1,909-observation dataset is the sole active analysis baseline on `main`.
It contains 955 white-like and 954 pigmented observations after joint environmental and predicted-*Bombus* support is applied. These values are checked before model fitting against `inputs/analysis_1909_expectations.csv`.

The former 1,923-observation analysis is archived under `legacy/published-1923/` and is not compared with the active analysis during ordinary execution.

## Ordered inferential design

| Stage | Question | Output role |
|---|---|---|
| 01 phenotype | Is visible pigmentation expressed, and how intense is colour among pigmented flowers? | Measurement and fixed upstream phenotype |
| 02 natural model | What national environmental and continuous spatial structure predicts the two response parts? | Cross-fitted INLA-SPDE baseline and 1,000 predictive maps |
| 03 local *Bombus* | Does nearby predicted-community turnover correspond to nearby flower-colour turnover? | Pre-specified 25-km local test with sensitivity radii |
| 04 local isolates | How often does a pigmented cell occur among environment-similar white neighbours? | Candidate count and fraction replayed on all natural maps |
| 05 human context | After candidates are fixed, are they directionally associated with population, DID, land use, or roads? | Exploratory, multiplicity-controlled characterization |
| 06 lock | Did every generating, validation, audit, and claim-control step complete? | Final manifests and claim registry |

Human variables never enter candidate selection. Flowering date does not enter the main claims. The reverse colour-state analysis and candidate DOY check are supplementary diagnostics only.

## Expected primary outputs

The exact Monte Carlo values are generated by each run. The reference run used to revise the manuscript produced:

- 18 local pigmented-isolate candidates;
- candidate count upper-tail p about 0.14;
- candidate fraction near the upper edge of the natural-map distribution (about 0.05 in the reference run);
- positive 25-km pigmentation-state turnover correspondence with the predicted *Bombus* fingerprint;
- weaker pigmented-only intensity turnover correspondence; and
- positive but familywise-inconclusive population/DID contrasts.

Because INLA posterior sampling is not guaranteed bitwise identical across machines and runs, these are scientific reference quantities, not exact regression locks. The active validation checks structure, finiteness, direction, fixed definitions, candidate identity, multiplicity handling, and causal claim ceilings.

## Interpretation ceiling

The national models estimate conditional environmental clines and unresolved continuous geography. The local *Bombus* analysis tests correspondence with predicted community turnover, not pollinator-mediated selection. Population and DID associations do not identify planting, garden escape, or introgression. Those require field observation, provenance records, vouchers, and genetic assignment.
