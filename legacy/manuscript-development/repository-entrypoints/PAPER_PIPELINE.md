# Paper pipeline — start here

This branch separates the analysis used by the current JBI paper from exploratory history.

## The paper in one line

**YAMAP recreational image stream → supervised quantitative flower-colour phenotype → broad environment + continuous spatial geography → 5-km focal-Bombus sharp-transition test → calibrated local departure events → post-selection human context.**

The scientific integration is sequential. Each layer creates the defensible baseline or comparison unit required by the next. The paper therefore **connects scales rather than collapsing them into one national omnibus regression**.

## Canonical scientific path

1. Begin with all 1,965 curated source rows in `Data_S1.csv`.
2. Reconstruct the current phenotype and broad/anomaly inputs with `scripts/run_reanalysis_current_inputs.sh`.
3. Continue adopted downstream natural-model, departure and human-context stages with `scripts/run_downstream_current_inputs.sh`.
4. Use the broad environment + SPDE result as the national natural template; do not interpret the spatial field as a named mechanism.
5. Build occurrence-referenced support for the documented widespread focal pollinators, *B. ardens* and *B. diversus*.
6. Run `scripts/run_bombus_local_sharp_transition.R`: the adopted Main pollinator test uses five nearest neighbours within 5 km, pure white↔pigmented transitions, Bombus-blind/sign-blind pair selection, greedy non-overlap, and post-selection white→pigmented orientation.
7. Keep five-species Hellinger community-boundary correspondence and the montane/equal-elevation test in Supporting Information as biogeographic context/guardrails rather than competing Main mechanisms.
8. Define locally discordant pigmented events without human predictors, replay the identical event under cross-fitted and joint natural predictive maps, then characterize human context only after candidate fixation.
9. Integrate the adopted outputs with `.github/workflows/final-paper-analysis.yml` and validate the JBI package with `.github/workflows/jbi-submission-format.yml`.

## Current Main-2 claim ceiling

The focal Bombus result is **weak, local and exploratory**. The occurrence-referenced 5-km sharp-transition design asks whether the sharpest nearby white→pigmented boundaries tend to point toward greater availability of the documented widespread effective Bombus pollinators. It is not an abundance, visitation, pollen-transfer or pollinator-mediated-selection estimate.

The five-species maximum is not the Main exposure because widespread/lowland and montane/alpine taxa replace one another geographically. Pooling all five would change the estimand from focal-pollinator opportunity to Bombus niche turnover. The equal-elevation montane analysis is retained specifically to show that visually striking high-elevation overlap does not provide an additional independent mechanism.

## Interpretation ceiling for the whole paper

- **YAMAP:** a complementary observation process and unusually dense same-period focal-species image source in the locked benchmark; not an unbiased survey or universally superior platform.
- **Colour phenotype:** CIELAB values are visible optical traits, not anthocyanin concentration, UV reflectance or Bombus receptor contrast.
- **Broad environment + space:** conditional geographical structure and predictive baseline, not proof of adaptation or a uniquely identified historical process.
- **Bombus:** predicted ecological opportunity/support, not observed visitation or selection.
- **Community turnover:** supplementary biogeographic correspondence without directional colour mechanism.
- **Human context:** characterization of pre-defined, natural-model-calibrated field/provenance targets; no horticultural-origin conclusion.

## What belongs in `legacy/`

A file belongs in `legacy/` when it is retained to explain **how the adopted design was reached**, but is not needed to reconstruct, run, validate, interpret or submit the current Main/Supp analyses.

Examples:

- fixed-1,909 and fixed-1,923 analysis identities;
- the old all-five lower-third limitation gate;
- national Bombus-fingerprint-as-Main formulations;
- superseded relaxation/effective-availability prototypes that preceded the adopted 5-km sharp-transition design;
- old E&E manuscripts and figure plans;
- obsolete reconstruction/resume wrappers;
- one-off diagnostics and result registries tied to superseded claims.

**Do not move the adopted sharp-transition analysis, the spatially matched five-species Supplement analysis, the montane guardrail, the YAMAP benchmark, or the JBI narrative/background architecture into legacy.** They are current paper evidence or current submission logic.

`legacy/` is provenance, **not an import path**. Active paper code and submission validators must not source executable material from it.
