# Supported environmental distance versus spatial continuity

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: fixed design; result pending checksum-locked execution

The final environment + SPDE models are not refitted. They are used only to identify terms whose 95% credible intervals exclude zero.

- pigmentation state distance: absolute difference in Temperature PC1;
- conditional-intensity distance: unweighted Euclidean distance across precipitation PC1, temperature seasonality, topography PC1 and the standardized Temperature PC1 × temperature-seasonality interaction.

Within each of five held-out geographical folds, pairs are stratified into five geographical-distance bins. Phenotype divergence in the upper versus lower supported-environment-distance quartile is compared with the identical statistic generated by 500 posterior-predictive maps from an intercept + stationary Matérn SPDE.

Exactly two tests are produced. Coefficients do not weight environmental distance. Direction is read from the final model; the supporting test is unsigned and does not establish selection, adaptation, genetics, drift, plasticity or direct physiology.
