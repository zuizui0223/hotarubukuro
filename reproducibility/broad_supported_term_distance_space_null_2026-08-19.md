# Supported environmental-term distance versus spatial continuity

Date: 2026-08-19  
Status: fixed design; result pending checksum-locked execution

## Question

Do locations separated more strongly along the environmental terms supported by the final response-specific model also differ more in flower colour than continuous spatial similarity alone predicts at comparable geographical separation?

This is deliberately simpler than rebuilding or coefficient-weighting the final model. The final observation-level environment + SPDE model remains primary and supplies the fixed set of supported environmental terms. The supporting test then uses ordinary pairwise environmental distance in those terms.

## Fixed environmental distances

### Pigmentation state

The final state model has one measured environmental term whose 95% credible interval excludes zero:

- Temperature PC1.

The pairwise environmental distance is therefore:

`absolute difference in training-fold-standardised Temperature PC1`.

### Conditional intensity

The final conditional-intensity model has four supported measured terms:

- precipitation PC1;
- temperature seasonality;
- topography PC1;
- Temperature PC1 × temperature-seasonality interaction.

The interaction is represented directly by the product `Temperature PC1 × temperature seasonality`, standardised on the training folds. The pairwise environmental distance is the Euclidean distance across those four training-fold-standardised terms.

No posterior coefficient is used as a weight. No full environment + SPDE model is refitted in this supporting analysis. Direction remains a property of the accepted full-model coefficients, whereas the distance test is unsigned.

## Spatial-continuity comparison

For each response and each of the five existing geographical folds:

1. fit an intercept + stationary Matérn SPDE model to the other four folds;
2. generate 500 posterior-predictive phenotype maps for the held-out fold;
3. construct up to 15,000 held-out cell pairs;
4. divide the pairs into five equal-count geographical-distance strata;
5. within each stratum, contrast phenotype divergence between the upper and lower quartiles of the response-specific supported-term environmental distance;
6. compare the observed mean contrast across the 25 fold-by-distance strata with the identical statistic from the space-only posterior-predictive maps.

The one-sided test asks whether the observed high-environment-distance minus low-environment-distance phenotype divergence is larger than expected from spatial continuity alone.

## Interpretation

A positive result means that separation along the environmental terms already supported by the final model orders held-out phenotype divergence beyond the expectation generated by fitted continuous geography.

It does not establish selection, local adaptation, genetic differentiation, developmental plasticity or direct anthocyanin physiology. The spatial field remains unresolved geography and may include population history, dispersal, unmeasured environment and sampling geometry.
