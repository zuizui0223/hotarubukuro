# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: biogeography of flower-colour polymorphism from hiking photographs.” Using screened YAMAP images, we reconstructed a national two-part flower-colour phenotype and matched each hypothesis to an appropriate spatial comparison. Temperature-associated pigmentation-state divergence exceeded fixed spatial continuity, whereas conditional intensity did not. Local Bombus correspondence was heterogeneous, and isolated pigmented occurrences were more population-exposed than natural maps predicted after density correction. The study shows how repurposed images, spatial models, local boundaries and natural-map replay can separate environmental, biotic and human-context layers without overstating adaptation, pollination or provenance.
