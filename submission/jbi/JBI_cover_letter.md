# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: flower-colour biogeography from hiking photographs.” Geographical intraspecific variation is ecologically important because it links local adaptation, population persistence and species interactions, yet abiotic, biotic, historical and human processes often share the same geography. Each component of our study is independently substantive, but the manuscript integrates them as one dependent biogeographical argument: recreational imagery enables a quantitative national phenotype; broad environment and space define its natural template; that template motivates a scale-shifted pollinator test and calibrates local departures before human-context inference. The conceptual contribution is integration across ecological scales, not an omnibus predictor model.
