# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: flower-colour biogeography from hiking photographs.” Floral colour polymorphism provides a tractable test of how physiological, reproductive, historical and contemporary processes combine across a species range. Using author-screened hiking photographs, we reconstruct a national quantitative phenotype, show that pigmentation state and intensity have different environmental organization, test a weak local pollinator-maintenance hypothesis, and natural-map calibrate local departures before examining human context. The conceptual advance is that macroecological trait geography can refine where adaptive, demographic and biotic mechanisms should next be tested directly.
