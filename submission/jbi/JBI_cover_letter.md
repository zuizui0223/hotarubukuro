# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: biogeography of flower-colour polymorphism from hiking photographs.” We converted screened YAMAP images into a national two-part flower-colour phenotype and used scale-matched comparisons to separate environmental, spatial, local Bombus and human-context layers. Pigmentation state showed a cool-climate association and held-out divergence beyond spatial continuity. Local Bombus correspondence was heterogeneous, while isolated pigmented occurrences were more population-exposed than natural maps predicted after density correction. The conceptual advance is integration across scales without promoting association to adaptation, pollination or provenance.
