# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: flower-colour biogeography from hiking photographs.” Each component is independently substantive—repurposed recreational imagery for quantitative traits, range-wide environment-plus-space modelling, a scale-shifted pollinator test, and event-based local departures—but the paper is designed as one dependent biogeographical argument. The image stream makes the national phenotype possible; broad spatial structure reveals why national pollinator overlap is ambiguous; that ambiguity motivates local boundary tests; and the natural template defines which departures merit human-context follow-up. This linked design, rather than an omnibus predictor model, is the manuscript’s central conceptual contribution.
