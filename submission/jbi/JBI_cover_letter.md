# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: flower-colour biogeography from hiking photographs.” The study asks how range-wide trait geography can be resolved without forcing environmentally structured abiotic and biotic predictors into one model. Using 1,922 author-reviewed GPS-linked hiking photographs, we derive a two-part flower-colour phenotype, establish a national environment–space template, then change scale to test local pollinator availability and change inferential object to identify repeatable local departures. We believe this mechanism-oriented, multiscale framework will interest the broad biogeography readership.
