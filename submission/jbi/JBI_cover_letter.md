# Cover letter draft — Journal of Biogeography

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit our Research Article, “From broad geography to local boundaries: biogeography of flower-colour polymorphism from hiking photographs.” Using screened YAMAP images, we built a national flower-colour dataset and resolved its geography into environmental, residual-spatial, local focal-Bombus and human-context layers. Our conceptual advance is scale-matched attribution: changing comparison units separates processes that national regression would confound. Continuous all-cell geometry shows that isolated pigmented occurrences are more population-exposed than natural maps predict, while density correction prevents overstatement of an apparent reciprocal white pattern. Positive, heterogeneous and null results generate testable physiological, reproductive and human-context hypotheses without assigning causality beyond the evidence.
