# JBI Main figure plan — four narrative figures

The Main story remains at four figures, but the visual progression is ecological rather than methodological: **measurement -> phenotypic layers -> broad candidate adaptive landscape -> local biotic maintenance hypothesis -> calibrated contemporary departures**. Each figure resolves one question and makes the next scale shift necessary. Panel labels follow JBI's lowercase (a)–(d) convention, and every map panel carries a 100-km bar scale.

## Figure 1 — From hiking photograph to two ecological layers of flower-colour polymorphism

**Narrative job:** reveal that the biological object is not one continuous pinkness axis. The reader first sees how a previously unmeasured national polymorphism is recovered, then sees that pigmentation state and intensity after pigmentation are distinct responses whose ecology can diverge.

Panels:

(a) YAMAP hiking activity -> GPS-linked photograph -> author organism/flower/ROI check -> RGB/CIELAB -> pigmentation state + conditional intensity;
(b) national locations of the 1,922 final observations;
(c) response-blind a* mixture and white/pigmented boundary;
(d) pigmented-only intensity as a separate ecological layer rather than a continuation of white-regime variation.

The full YAMAP/iNaturalist/GBIF benchmark remains Supporting Information. The visual payoff is biological: the paper will ask separately what governs **whether pigmentation is expressed** and **how strong visible colour becomes once expressed**.

## Figure 2 — Broad environmental and residual geographic structure

**Narrative job:** establish the candidate macroecological landscape in which the polymorphism is organized, while showing why broad geographical association cannot by itself be equated with local adaptation or population history.

Panels:

(a) final observation-level environmental associations: additive pigmentation-state model and conditional-intensity model with Temperature PC1 × temperature seasonality;
(b) cross-fitted pigmentation-state geography from the final eight-axis natural predictive reference;
(c) cross-fitted conditional-intensity geography;
(d) residual spatial ranges (state ≈132.8 km; intensity ≈65.7 km) and geographically blocked predictive performance.

The figure should make two biological points visible: state and intensity have different environmental organization, and substantial unexplained regional continuity remains after measured environment. That unresolved geography becomes a future population-genomic target rather than being labelled post hoc as history. VPD/SWB and coastline-barrier alternatives remain claim-bounding diagnostics.

## Figure 3 — Zoom to local boundaries for a pollinator-maintenance hypothesis

**Narrative job:** change resolution after the Broad map has made same-scale biotic overlap ambiguous. The question is no longer whether national flower-colour and Bombus maps overlap, but whether abrupt local pigmentation-state boundaries correspond directionally to focal-pollinator opportunity.

Panels:

(a) Bombus-blind, environment-blind 5-km pure white–pigmented transition design (67 non-overlapping pairs);
(b) finalized eight-axis Broad environmental-balance diagnostic for the already fixed pairs: selected median RMS distance 0.244 versus 0.318 for generic 5-km graph edges;
(c) pigmented-minus-white occurrence-referenced max(*B. ardens*, *B. diversus*) contrast;
(d) claim ceiling: median near zero, 49.3% positive, q=0.0815 across 5/10/25-km pure tests, attenuation at 10/25 km and failure on raw SDM support.

Panel (b) is diagnostic only: environment did not select or weight pairs. High-elevation montane/alpine tests and five-species community turnover remain Appendix S5. The biological payoff is deliberately limited: if a Bombus contribution exists, the pattern fits **local maintenance/loss of pigmentation state** better than progressive darkening.

## Figure 4 — Natural-map calibration and contemporary provenance targets

**Narrative job:** resolve what a visually surprising local colour state means after the Broad natural geography is known. The figure should show that exceptional-looking populations need not be statistically exceptional under natural spatial variation, and that human context is a final overlay rather than a competing national driver.

Panels:

(a) event definition: pigmented cell with at least three neighbours within 10 km that are similar in the finalized eight-axis abiotic state space (RMS <=1) and contain only observed white flowers;
(b) map of **16** candidate field/provenance locations;
(c) observed candidate count and fraction against 10,000 final-eight-axis cross-fitted natural maps (count P=0.279; fraction upper-tail P=0.126);
(d) post-selection human-context family, highlighting 5-km population exposure (contrast +0.0674, directional P=0.0080, global maxT FWER P=0.0548) while retaining other scales/pathways for comparison.

Caption language should move the reader from apparent anomaly to testable provenance: these configurations are not excessive under the fitted natural model, while the weak settlement signal motivates field history and genomic comparison without establishing anthropogenic origin.

## Main-text figure rule

Robustness-only evidence stays in Supporting Information. Each Main figure must advance the ecological argument, not merely document a method. Weak and null evidence must remain visually explicit because the narrowing of hypotheses is itself part of the biological result.
